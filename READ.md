# Rest & Reflect
Rest and reflect is a simple yet effective tool that allows its users to do nightly check ins on the go and to reflect the incidents they experience during the day.


## Problem
A lot of indviduals in the world battle the daily stressors of life. Some individuals more than other to the point of developing mental health problems, this effective add on tool will help users have a go to supportive app free of charge that allows them process their thoughts and decide on the next appropriate corrective step in times of distress.


## Current Features
Input functions on python that takes responses from users
It saves the responses to a text file
